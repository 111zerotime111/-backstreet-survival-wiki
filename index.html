🐱 Cat Ritual

Secret Activity: The Cat Ritual requires finding four hidden cats and taking them to the secret ritual location.

1 Find the 4 Hidden Cats 🐱

Explore the town and alleys to locate four hidden cat figures. They are typically tucked away in hard-to-see spots.

  • Search behind buildings.
  • Check narrow alleys.
  • Look along exterior walls.
  • Search near trash piles.

When you spot a cat figure, walk right up to it and interact with it to pick it up.

Once collected, the cat will be sent ahead to the ritual site.

2 Head to the Ritual Location 🔮

Once you have collected all four cats, navigate to the secret ritual spot.

3 Activate the Ritual & Claim Your Reward 🎁

  1. Place the Cats: At the ritual site, interact with the environment to trigger the cat event.
  2. Open the Chest: Completing the ritual will spawn a secret reward chest on the ground.
  3. Equip Your Skin: Open the chest to unlock your unique item skin. Open your in-game inventory/backpack menu, switch to the Skins tab, and equip it.

Your trash-digging wand will now flash and be covered in glowing pink cat paw prints! 🐾
