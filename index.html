Overview

Backstreet Survival: Enhanced is a survival/economy-style Roblox game where players collect trash, make money, craft items, fish, cook, garden, use shops, explore dangerous areas, and build up their wealth.

This wiki records player-confirmed information and facts provided by players. Exact prices, odds, requirements, and mechanics that have not been confirmed are marked as unknown rather than guessed.

Wiki rule: Player-reported mechanics are labeled when the exact official value has not been independently verified.

Rarities

The currently documented rarity ladder is:

Common → Uncommon → Rare → Epic → Legendary → Mythic

Mythic is currently the highest documented rarity. Exact drop rates are not currently known.

Activities

Trash Digging

Search trash piles for items and sell the items for money.

Trash piles can contain items with these rarities:

Common Uncommon Rare Epic Legendary Mythic

Bean Factory

Make beans at the Bean Factory to earn money.

You need at least $80 to buy the clothing needed to work there.

Fishing

Catch fish, cook the fish, and sell the cooked fish to people for money.

Begging

Beg on the side of the street for money.

People may ignore you, meaning you can receive no money.

In-game mechanic: Player-reported gameplay also says a baseball bat can be used to rob people after begging interactions. This is documented only as an in-game mechanic.

Gardening

Grow plants and collect them before they decay.

If a plant decays before it is picked up, the money from that plant is lost.

Rats can eat plants.

Killing a rat while gardening gives $1 per rat.

Shops

Bean Shop

The Bean Shop can be robbed at certain times.

  • A notification appears when the Bean Shop becomes available to rob.
  • A notification appears whenever the shop is being robbed.
  • A baseball bat can be used to smash items inside.
  • Loot from smashed items can be sold.
  • The shop owner has a turret.
  • The baseball bat costs $5.

Poor Store

The Poor Store sells:

  • New/better trash-picking tools
  • Trash bags
  • Batteries
  • Lottery tickets
  • Cardboard hat
  • Homeless signs
  • Shirts/T-shirts

Lottery

Each lottery ticket costs $1. The documented chance to win is 1 in 100, with a $100 prize.

Locations

Beach

  • Fishing is available.
  • Crates float around in the ocean.
  • Sharks are present.
  • Trash piles can be searched.
  • Epic Treasure Chests can be dug up from beach trash piles.
  • Opening an Epic Treasure Chest costs $10.
  • Beach access requires a one-time $100 ticket.

Sewer

  • Contains trash piles.
  • Has toxic air.
  • A gas mask is required.
  • The gas mask costs around $300.
  • An alarm can signal an incoming wave.
  • Players should quickly climb the ladder into the safe zone.
  • A large wave comes through the Sewer.
  • Players outside the safe zone can be swept away.

Bank

The Bank allows players to deposit and withdraw cash and view transaction logs.

Deposited cash earns interest.

Interest is credited every 24 hours.

The exact interest rate is currently unknown.

Bean Shop

Bean Shop is the main location associated with Bean Shop robbery mechanics.

Players can work here making beans after purchasing the required clothing.

Chaos Events

Airstrike

Price: 79 Robux

Bombers attack the entire city.

Players caught in a blast are eliminated in-game.

Blackout

Price: 99 Robux

  • Night only.
  • Enables PvP for everyone.
  • Lights turn off.
  • Described as "no laws, pure anarchy."

Rebirth

Rebirth resets the player's cash/statistics in exchange for the rebirth.

  • No money reward is given for rebirthing.
  • Cash/statistics reset.
  • Skins remain.
  • Game passes remain.
  • Roblox Plus benefits remain.
  • VIP server access remains.

Required cash: Exact requirement not currently provided.

Humanitarian Aid

Humanitarian Aid provides free food and free money every day.

To receive it, players must:

  • Join the game group.
  • Like the game.

Helipad

You need at least $50,000 in your wallet to fly.

The $50,000 is not spent. It is a flex/status requirement.

Road Hazards & Cannon

Traffic

Crossing the road to reach areas such as the Beach, Bean Shop, or Bank can be dangerous.

Getting hit by a car instantly kills/eliminates the character.

Cannon Guy

The Cannon Guy provides a shortcut across the road.

Cost: $5

The cannon launches the player across the road, allowing them to bypass traffic.

Selling Crafted Items

TVs and other crafted items can be sold to passersby.

  1. Go onto the street.
  2. Equip the TV or other crafted item.
  3. Sell it to a passerby.

This applies to crafted electronics and other crafted items.

Secrets & Weird Facts

Poop Damage

Slipping on poop in front of the Bean Shop deals 50 damage.

Falling Damage

Falling from a building deals approximately 15–20 damage.

That means the documented poop hazard can deal more damage than falling from a building.

AFK Sleeping

If a player stays AFK for long enough, their character eventually starts sleeping on the floor.

If the player moves while sleeping, the character screams while getting up.

Rats

Rats can eat garden plants.

Killing a rat while gardening gives $1.

Baseball Bat

The baseball bat has multiple documented in-game uses, including smashing Bean Shop objects and killing rats.

Important Mechanics

Trash Tools

Trash-picking tools are not simply upgraded through a linear upgrade system. Better/new tools are purchased at the Poor Store.

Garden Plants

Plants need to be picked up before they decay. Once a plant decays before pickup, the associated money is lost.

VIP Greenhouse

The Greenhouse License prevents rats only inside the VIP plot. It does not prevent rats from appearing outside it.
