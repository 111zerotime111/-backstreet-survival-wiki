📖 Game Overview

Backstreet Survival: Enhanced is a Roblox survival/simulator experience associated with Hoshineko Studio.

The developer information documented by this wiki identifies @1lia_a as the creator, developer, and maintainer.

The game's central theme involves surviving in a poor town, digging through trash, completing activities, and gradually working your way out of poverty.

The Enhanced version is described as a remaster/reimagining connected to the original Backstreet Survival experience.

Current documented version: Poverty Edition 3.6.
Last recorded update: 01.10.2026.
